Before you enter Camp Canary, you can ask the people who live nearby what they know.

You decide to talk to some locals about Camp Canary before venturing into the camp itself. Some things people will tell you without prompting. Other things require a bit of investigation.

No one is lying.

They’re telling their story.

How it works: choose any category. You can switch categories whenever you want. Hints must be taken in order within each category. Hints 1 and 2 are free. Hints 3 and 4 each cost +1 Investigation Point. Record paid hints on your Investigation Point Tracker. Revealed hints may always be reread for free.
